## The mechanism as I am modelling it

A worker loops yarn over the five knobs surrounding one chosen face, works
successive rounds by lifting each loop over the standing yarn, and the finished
tube passes through the aperture at the centre of that face into the hollow
interior. The twelve faces carry apertures of different diameters, so one
object is a set of twelve gauges; the face selected sets the diameter of the
tube produced. The object is a hand-held domestic textile tool. Nothing else in
the mechanism is load-bearing, heated, rotated, or metrological.

## Declaration

### Which variables were hardest to specify, and why?

1. **EV017 and EV042 (wear from fibre on bronze).** The direction is clear —
   wear at the knob necks and aperture lips — but the *magnitude* is not. Soft
   wool at hand tension is a slow abrasive, and I cannot say from the mechanism
   alone whether a lifetime of household use leaves wear visible without
   microscopy. I predicted the pattern and left the amount unstated, which
   makes these cells vulnerable to a scorer who reads them as predicting
   conspicuous wear.
2. **EV026 (province).** The mechanism is genuinely province-neutral, which
   pushes towards `0`, but neutrality plus a universal craft entails a broad
   distribution, which makes a tight regional concentration mildly contrary. I
   took the second reading and wrote `-`. A reasonable specifier would write
   `0` here, and I expect this to be a disagreement cell.
3. **EV045 (manufacturing difficulty).** This is the mechanism's weakest point
   and the hardest place to obey the "strongest proponent" instruction without
   crossing into advocacy. I recorded it as contrary and stated the best
   defence rather than adopting it.
4. **EV005 (opposed apertures).** Depends on whether the tube is left in the
   cavity or drawn out of the opposite hole. Both readings give "not matched",
   which is why I could still write a directional prediction, but the
   confidence behind it is lower than the symbol suggests.
5. **EV024 (residues).** A prediction whose failure is ambiguous: absence of
   fibre is equally well explained by two millennia of burial, so the `+` is
   near-unfalsifiable in the negative direction while strongly confirmatory in
   the positive.

### Which three predictions do you consider most decisive?

1. **EV041 — knob wear.** Every stitch drags fibre over a knob neck. This is
   the one place the mechanism generates a wear signature that no other
   proposed function generates in the same location, and it is the prediction I
   would stake the hypothesis on.
2. **EV004 with EV043 — graded, distinguishable apertures within one object.**
   If apertures on a single object are not systematically different, the
   twelve-gauge reading collapses and there is no reason for twelve faces.
3. **EV027 — association with textile equipment.** A domestic textile tool that
   never once co-occurs with the rest of a textile kit across the whole corpus
   is a tool without a workshop.

The most decisive *negative* is **EV045**: the mismatch between the trivial
requirement (a ring of pegs around a hole) and the elaborate, expensive object
actually produced. Nothing in the mechanism explains why this had to be cast
bronze rather than turned wood.

### Before any evidence is consulted: how well do you expect this hypothesis to do?

**Moderately — better on morphology than on context, and worse overall than the
prompt's aside about it "currently leading" would imply.**

Specifically, I expect it to score well on the form variables (EV004, EV007,
EV008, EV009, EV034, EV043), because the mechanism was reverse-engineered from
exactly those features and they are the object's most conspicuous properties.

I expect it to do badly on four things, and I would not be surprised if it
lost on all four:

- **EV045** — I think the manufacturing cost is a real and unanswered problem.
- **EV027 / EV024** — I doubt the corpus records textile-tool associations or
  fibre residues at any useful rate, and I expect these to be recorded as
  absent or unknown rather than confirmed.
- **EV031 / EV025** — my sense of the mechanism says domestic; if the corpus is
  weighted towards military and rural contexts, EV025, EV026 and EV031 fail
  together as a correlated block, and those three should not be counted as
  three independent hits or three independent misses.
- **EV041** — the prediction I consider most decisive is also the one I am
  least confident the corpus can answer, because it needs microwear data that
  probably does not exist for most specimens.

So: a hypothesis that fits the shape of the object well, fits its cost and its
findspots poorly, and whose single decisive test is probably unmeasured. If it
is currently leading, I would want to know how much of that lead comes from the
morphology block — which any hypothesis reverse-engineered from the object's
form will win — and how much survives once the correlated context variables are
collapsed.
